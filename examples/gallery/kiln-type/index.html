Kiln Type — Grotto Sans

Grotto Sans is a grotesque cut for rooms with bad light: open counters, a tall x-height and terminals that stay sharp at small sizes. It began as lettering for a cave museum's signs and grew into a family of nine weights, each with an italic.

The drawing is plain on purpose. Grotto does not want to be noticed on the wall; it wants the wall to be read. Variable weight and width, 640 glyphs, tabular figures and a stencil set for the days when the signs are sprayed.
