One night on tape

The log of a fictional recorder, replayed line by line. Three sites between 02:00 and 05:00: the third bridge, the river steps and the tram depot.

One mark says silence: the level fell under the recorder's own noise, so there was nothing left to keep.

$ rec start --site bridge-03 --gain 42
level  -47 dB  wind=2m/s  temp=9C  02:04
heard  tram, far side, heading to the depot  02:06
heard  traffic hum, steady, no single car  02:09
level  -49 dB  02:15
heard  a door, three streets away  02:21
$ rec stop
level  saved bridge-03.wav  17m 02s
# ... walked down to the river steps ...
$ rec start --site river-steps --gain 48
heard  water against stone, every 4 s  02:41
heard  a bicycle chain, one pass  02:44
level  -52 dB  02:46
heard  gulls, two, arguing  02:58
level  -55 dB  03:10
$ rec stop
level  saved river-steps.wav  31m 40s
# ... to the tram depot, after the last car ...
$ rec start --site tram-depot --gain 54
heard  overhead wire ticking as it cools  03:52
heard  ticking slows, every 9 s  04:05
level  -58 dB  04:12
level  -61 dB  04:20
$ rec mark silence
silence  -63 dB, under the recorder's own noise  04:26
heard  first bus warming up  04:51
$ rec stop
level  saved tram-depot.wav  58m 12s
$ rec summary
level  3 sites  1h 47m  11 heard  1 silence

Sites

Heard

Not kept
