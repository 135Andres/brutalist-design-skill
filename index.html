brutalist

Screenshot in, brutalist site out. Give your coding AI a picture of a raw, experimental website and it rebuilds it as a working page — motion included — or uses it as a springboard for something new. It measures instead of guessing, and says what it invented.

Commands

/brutalist <command> <target>
specifiedrecreate <image>

Rebuild a reference as a working page: rights, inventory, build, comparison, report.

under reviewmotion <page>

Plan and build motion; from a still image it is declared invention.

experimentalinspire <images…>

Divergent sketches and visual questions, taking principles, never copies.

experimentaledit <page>

The smallest change first; escalate only if it is not enough.

not specifiedverify <page>

Screenshots under fixed conditions, comparison, accessibility pass.

not specifiedcritique <page>

A review without scores. The human judges.
